A Buyer's Guide

How to Choose Your Model

Three questions to narrow it down. Each model is built to the same standard — the choice is really about how you want to live.

01

Two-storey or bungalow?

Bungalows put all primary living on one level — ideal for downsizers or anyone preferring step-free everyday flow. Two-storey homes maximize square footage on the same lot footprint.

02

How many bedrooms do you need?

Bedroom counts range from 2 to 5 across our models. Consider home offices, guest rooms, or multi-generational living when narrowing your range.

03

Which elevation feels like home?

Each model is offered in Elevation A and B — same interior layout, different exterior architectural treatment. Click any model above to switch between them.
